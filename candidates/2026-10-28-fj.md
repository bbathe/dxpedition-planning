## License

Nothing has been applied for. The wanted issued call is a TO special. A personal FJ4 call is not the product ANFR describes for a one-week visitor. The FCC license class is not in `operator.md`.

**Path to a TO call.** Saint-Barthélemy sits in the TO block for special temporary calls. That is the 6 June 2021 text of Annex II to the arrêté of 21 September 2000, on Légifrance, seen 28 October 2026. The prefix is TO, then one to three digits, then a suffix of one to four characters ending in a letter. A requested suffix is a wish. ANFR assigns the call. The use is limited to 15 days in a six-month period. Eight through 15 March 2027 is eight days.

The request has to be motivated. ANFR’s indicatif page describes these calls for international gatherings, exceptional events, and contests (`https://www.anfr.fr/gerer/radioamateurs/les-indicatifs`). Whether a one-week activation qualifies is ANFR’s decision.

Do this before sending an identity document. The United States does not issue a HAREC certificate. ARRL’s CEPT page, seen 28 October 2026, says the US is not in CEPT T/R 61-02, so a US license does not by itself produce a full license in a HAREC country (`https://www.arrl.org/cept`). The arrêté says a foreign operator must supply a HAREC certificate or an equivalent, the current foreign license, and an identity document. Ask the Saint-Dié desk whether they will accept the FCC license grant as that equivalent. The February 2023 form prints the desk phone as 03 29 42 20 74. If they will not, this issued-call path stops there.

If they will, file at `https://teleservice-amateurs.anfr.fr`, or mail the February 2023 form to ANFR, DLC, 4 rue Alphonse Matter, BP 8314, 88108 Saint-Dié-des-Vosges Cedex (`https://www.anfr.fr/fileadmin/Radioamateurs/DEMANDE_D_INDICATIF_SPECIAL_TEMPORAIRE.pdf`). The form asks for the operator’s call, the call requested, the address of use, WGS84 coordinates, a motivation, the dates, and the operator list. Attach the paper they accepted in place of HAREC, the current FCC license, and an identity document. The form says the dates are not modifiable after filing, and that filing does not mean the call will be issued.

The current ANFR page and the arrêté both say the request must be filed at least 20 working days before use. Counting weekdays only, that date for a start on Monday 8 March 2027 is Monday 8 February 2027. The form’s sentence is different: the request must arrive “dans les 30 jours avant” the start. Ask the desk which window they enforce. Have the request in their hands by 8 February 2027 unless they say otherwise.

**Guest call, if the TO call is not issued.** For a stay under three months, ANFR says a foreign amateur from a T/R 61-01 country needs no formalities. The call is FJ, then the home call, then P, M, or MM, so FJ/AD8FD/P if the license qualifies. ARRL says full CEPT guest privileges under T/R 61-01 are for US Extra and Advanced. General is a limited class under ECC (05)06, and only in countries that adopted it. Technician and Novice are not in CEPT. Carry the original FCC license, proof of US citizenship, and FCC Public Notice DA 16-1048. That guest call is not an issued TO call.

Scheduled hops into SBH can publish two 50 lb pieces on paper. On both airlines below, the second piece is space-available, so the station is not assured on the same aircraft. Anything over 62 linear inches fails both rules. March 2027 seats were not opened.

The excess-baggage page says second and third bags are standby, and a bag over 50 lb up to 70 lb is also space-available. Nothing over 70 lb. Passengers are generally limited to two checked pieces. - Cape Air, STT–SBH, on its own baggage page fetched 28 October 2026 (`https://www.capeair.com/baggage/`). They operate 9-passenger aircraft and say additional pieces may not fit. A local Caribbean ticket includes one gate-checked bag up to 45 linear inches, not a free 50 lb suitcase. One more standard bag up to 50 lb and 62 inches is USD 40. **Forecast.** These are climate descriptions, not a forecast for March 2027.
