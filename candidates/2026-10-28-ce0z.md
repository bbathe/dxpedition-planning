# Juan Fernández candidate

Saved 2026-10-28. This note is not an announcement, a booking, or a permit. No `expeditions/` folder until an entity and a year are chosen and a license and landing path are real.

## Window

**Assumption:** solo or two operators, one station. About seven days on site. Travel sits immediately around the on-site week, so door-to-door is longer. Departure airports are CVG and DAY. Home city is Lebanon, Ohio.

The asked window was a one-week activation inside 1–14 March 2027. July and August are also travel months. They are the worse months for this entity.

ARRL DX Phone runs 0000 UTC 6 March through 2359 UTC 7 March 2027. A phone station that weekend only works W/VE stations.

## Travel

Gateway is Santiago (SCL), then Robinson Crusoe airfield, then about an hour by boat to San Juan Bautista in Cumberland Bay. Chile’s tourism site describes that air-plus-boat path. The airstrip is at the southwest end of the island. There is no road to town.

Aerolíneas ATA, on its own site, sells tourist seats from November through April. The fare includes 10 kg of baggage and the boat. Resident subsidized flights run all year. Those are not a tourist booking. No July or August 2027 tourist schedule was published. A winter charter is TBD.

Aerocardal’s January 2025 terms PDF lists a round trip at USD 1,500, 10 kg of baggage, Santiago departure 10:30, and the return at 14:00, including the boat transfer. That is a 2025 document, not a 2027 booking. Flight days for 2027 are TBD. The same sheet says to allow one day in Santiago or on the island between a commercial flight and the island flight, because weather delays are common.

FlightsFrom, checked 28 October 2026, lists Delta Atlanta–Santiago daily, about 9 hours 5 minutes to 9 hours 35 minutes. The week it displays, 9–15 November 2026, shows DL147 at 20:30. The week list includes 1–7 March and 8–14 March 2027. March clock times were not opened. DL297 is also listed. An evening Atlanta departure on that November pattern arrives in Santiago the next morning.

Delta flies CVG–Atlanta about seven to nine times a day. DAY–Atlanta is about three or four flights a day on regional jets. Use CVG unless a specific Dayton connection is the one that makes the Atlanta flight. A morning landing at the main Santiago airport does not confirm a same-day 10:30 FBO departure. Plan the island flight for the day after landing in Santiago.

Routing from CVG or DAY is not a booking.

## Site / lodging

San Juan Bautista is the only town. Refugio Náutico, facing Cumberland Bay, is published at islarobinsoncrusoe.cl. Availability for March 2027 was not checked. There are no ATMs. Cash is required. One pharmacy and one rural hospital. The island tourism site says 99 percent of the territory is a national park, and pet entry is regulated by SAG and CONAF.

## Season

Chile’s tourism site says the warmer, drier season is November through March. The island tourism site says most visitors come from October through March, rain is mainly April through October, winter averages about 12°C, and summer averages about 19°C.

These are climate normals, not a forecast for 2027. The 1991–2020 normals for the Juan Fernández Islands station are from the Dirección Meteorológica de Chile, as shown on the Robinson Crusoe climate table checked 28 October 2026. Rainy-day counts on that table are the World Meteorological Organization’s 1981–2010 normals.

| | March | July | August | December |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Daily mean | 18.6°C | 12.8°C | 12.5°C | 17.1°C |
| Rainfall | 55.1 mm | 130.5 mm | 114.3 mm | 24.8 mm |
| Days with at least 1 mm | 8.8 | 15.9 | 13.5 | 5.4 |

March 2027 is still on Chile’s summer time. Decreto 98, published 2 July 2026, keeps continental Chile, which is the island’s time, on the one-hour advance from 24:00 on the first Saturday of September 2026 until 24:00 on the first Saturday of April 2027. That is UTC−3. July and August 2027 are UTC−4.

Cold is acceptable to the operator. The season still matters because rain and sea state cancel the boat and the strip, and because July and August are outside the published tourist flights.

## Propagation

**Forecast.** VOACAP Online, run 28 October 2026, short path from Robinson Crusoe to Lebanon, Ohio. The figure is the share of days in the month when the signal meets the mode threshold. **Assumption:** 100 W, a quarter-wave vertical over good ground on the island, a dipole at 15 m in Ohio, a quiet receive site, no sporadic-E, minimum takeoff angle 3°. A beam would raise the numbers. It would not restore 10 m in July. Good ground under the vertical is optimistic here.

Sunspot numbers are NOAA SWPC’s smoothed forecast, retrieved 28 October 2026: March 86, July 75, August 72, December 61. July’s range on that file is about 56 to 92.

Hours at or above 50 percent, CW, into Ohio:

In March the 10 m window is about 14:00–23:00 UTC and 00:00–01:00 UTC. In July the only 10 m hours at 50 percent are 00:00–01:00 UTC.

At 100 W, March SSB stays at or above 50 percent for 4 hours on 10 m, 8 hours on 12 m, and 6 hours on 15 m. July SSB never reaches 50 percent on 12 m or 10 m. A CW trip on 40, 30, and 20 m still works in July. A phone trip, or one that depends on 12 m and 10 m, does not.

CW to Frankfurt has 8 hours on 10 m in March and none in July. CW to Tokyo has 11 hours on 10 m in March and none in July. Europe keeps 17 m and 20 m in July.

December CW into Ohio is close to March on 12 m and 10 m and stronger on 40 m. Phone is a little shorter than March. December is inside the November–April tourist flights and is the driest month in the normals above.
