## License and landing

License: TBD. The operator will request J38FD. J38 is the visitor series on the society call-sign page (`https://grenadaamateurradio.blogspot.com/2018/06/grenada-call-sign-structure.html`). One, two, or three letters follow the prefix, and the whole call is not to exceed six characters, so J38FD fits. The one-letter condition on that page, Advanced-equivalent and Morse, applies to a call such as J38W, not to J38FD. The society says a J38 call is issued only at NTRC’s discretion, and otherwise the licence is J3 plus the home call (`https://grenadaamateurradio.blogspot.com/2015/11/grenada-amateur-radio-licence.html`). Checked 2 November 2026, J38FD does not appear on the IOTA Grenada list (`https://www.iota-world.org/islands-on-the-air/iota-groups-islands/539.html`) or the eQSL Grenada list (`http://www.eqsl.cc/qslcard/CountryList.cfm?LimitCountry=GRENADA`). Those are not the NTRC assignment list. The National Telecommunications Regulatory Commission procedure list at `https://ntrc.gd/ar-application-procedures/` requires the completed form, two passport photos, fees, equipment specifications, government photo identification, and the existing home amateur license. The fee page at `https://ntrc.gd/licensing-fees/` opened on 2 November 2026. Amateur radio, including a temporary call sign, is $25 to apply and $50 initial. The application form states the EC$25 is payable to the National Telecommunications Regulatory Commission and must accompany the form (`https://ntrc.gd/wp-content/uploads/2021/09/Class-Licences-Application-Form-for-Amateur-Radio-Licence.pdf`). Its May 2022 note says cash and credit cards are not allowed and a local cheque or bank draft is preferred (`https://grenadaamateurradio.blogspot.com/2022/05/visitors-amateur-radio-operators-on.html`). Its earlier payment list still includes cash, a foreign cheque, an international money order, and a wire, and says the bank takes about six weeks to clear a foreign cheque, money order, or bank draft (`https://grenadaamateurradio.blogspot.com/2016/11/how-to-get-visitors-grenada-wi-amateur.html`). ## Season

March is the dry season, not a booked weather window. At Maurice Bishop International Airport, the Caribbean Regional Climate Centre’s 1986–2014 record gives March an average of 27 mm, the driest month, with the dry-season peak in February through April.
