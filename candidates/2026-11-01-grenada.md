## License and landing

License: TBD. The operator will request J3/AD8FD and will not request a J38 call. The National Telecommunications Regulatory Commission procedure list at `https://ntrc.gd/ar-application-procedures/` requires the completed form, two passport photos, fees, equipment specifications, government photo identification, and the existing home amateur license. That page and the fee page returned an error on a direct open on 1 November 2026. The fee table indexed the same day lists amateur radio, including a temporary call sign, at EC$25 to apply and EC$50 initial. The Grenada Caribbean Amateur Radio Society states the same two amounts, payable separately to the NTRC and to the Government of Grenada, and says a J38 call is issued only at NTRC’s discretion. Otherwise the license is J3 plus the home call sign. The call printed on the license is still TBD until NTRC issues it.

A US tourist visa is not required. The State Department country page says a passport valid six months beyond entry, one blank page, and proof of onward travel, with entry granted for three months. Grenada’s tourism authority says the same for US citizens.

The US travel advisory is Level 2, issued 5 January 2026, for crime. It says violent crime can occur anywhere, and that police response is slower than in the United States. Driving is on the left. The same page recommends a local temporary driver’s license.

The one fact that would promote or drop the trip is an NTRC license in hand, plus written permission from the host to put antennas on the lawn.

## Season

March is the dry season, not a booked weather window. At Maurice Bishop International Airport, the Caribbean Regional Climate Centre’s 1986–2014 record gives March an average of 27 mm, the driest month, with the dry-season peak in February through April. That is a climate normal, not a forecast for March 2027. The State Department places the hurricane season in August through October, so this week is outside that season. Earthquakes and rip currents are year-round risks on that page.
